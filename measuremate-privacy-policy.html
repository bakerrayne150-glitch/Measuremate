MeasureMate — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

MeasureMate is a home-project material calculator. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. The short version: there are no accounts — no names, no emails, no sign-ups.

Information we store

Saved projects. When you tap Save, the project name, calculator type, your inputs, and the results are sent to our backend database (Supabase) and linked to a random anonymous ID created automatically on first launch. This is what lets your projects sync if you reinstall. We never see your name or email because we never ask for them.

On-device preferences only. Your affiliate store choice (Amazon / Home Depot / Lowe's), remembered material prices, and Pro unlock status are stored locally on your phone. They are never uploaded anywhere.

Third-party services

  • Supabase — hosts the backend database holding saved projects, tied only to the anonymous ID described above. See Supabase's privacy policy.
  • Google AdMob — displays banner ads to free users. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and ad interaction data under Google's policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses data from AdMob.
  • Google Play Billing — processes the optional $4.99 one-time Pro unlock. Payment details are handled entirely by Google; the app only learns whether Pro is active.

Information we do not collect

Names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, or sensor data. The app requests no special device permissions beyond internet access, which is required for project sync and ads.

Data retention and deletion

Saved projects remain in our database until you delete them in the app (Projects tab → delete). Uninstalling the app removes local data but not the cloud copy tied to your anonymous ID. To delete everything associated with your anonymous ID, contact us at the address below.

Children

MeasureMate is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
